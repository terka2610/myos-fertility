Renewing the storage of your eggs

What happens when your storage period is due for renewal?

Your eggs remain yours. You remain in control.

If your frozen eggs are stored through the MYOS centralised cryogenic storage service, you will be contacted before your current storage period is due to end. This leaflet explains how the renewal process is intended to work and how you can keep track of it.

1

We will remind you before renewal is due

MYOS will contact you using the contact details you have provided, in advance of your storage renewal date. The message will explain when your current storage period ends, what you need to do and the deadline for responding. Please keep your email address, telephone number and postal address up to date with MYOS.

2

You tell us what you would like to do

You will be asked to confirm your instructions. These may include renewing the storage of your eggs, arranging their transfer to another suitable storage provider or fertility clinic, or discussing another option available under your consent arrangements and applicable regulations. If you are unsure, contact MYOS before making a decision. Do not assume that storage will automatically renew.

3

MYOS coordinates with the storage facility and your chosen clinic

Once MYOS receives your completed instructions and any required payment or documentation, it will coordinate the next steps with the centralised storage facility. Where needed and with your authorisation, MYOS will also communicate with the fertility clinic of your choice. This may include confirming your details, sharing the relevant records and consent documents, and arranging any required transfer or ongoing storage instructions.

4

You receive confirmation and can monitor progress

MYOS will provide you with updates as your renewal is processed. You should receive confirmation when your instructions have been accepted and when the storage facility has confirmed the updated storage arrangements. If any information or action is outstanding, MYOS will let you know what is needed and who is responsible for the next step.

How you can keep track

MYOSYour main point of contact for renewal reminders, instructions and progress updates.
Storage facilityConfirms receipt of instructions and the status of the storage arrangements.
Your chosen fertility clinicReceives or provides information where you have authorised this and where it is needed for your care or transfer.
YouKeep your contact details current, respond by the stated deadline and retain the written confirmations.

For your own records, keep copies of the renewal notice, your instructions, any payment confirmation and MYOS’s written confirmation that the storage arrangements have been renewed. If you have not received an update by the date stated in your renewal communication, contact MYOS and ask for a progress update.

Frequently asked questions

Will my eggs be moved when I renew storage?
Not necessarily. A routine renewal may simply update the storage agreement and records. If a physical transfer is needed, MYOS will explain why and coordinate the arrangements with you, the storage facility and your chosen clinic, as appropriate.
Does my fertility clinic automatically receive updates?
MYOS will communicate with your chosen clinic when this is required and you have provided the relevant authorisation. You can ask MYOS which information will be shared and with whom.
What if I do not want to renew?
Contact MYOS as soon as possible so that you can discuss the available options and any steps or deadlines that apply. Your eggs will be managed in accordance with your consent, the storage agreement and applicable regulations.
What if my contact details change?
Tell MYOS promptly so that important reminders and time-sensitive information can reach you.
You can ask MYOS for an update at any stage of the renewal process.
